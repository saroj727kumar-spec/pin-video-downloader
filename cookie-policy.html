Cookie Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Browsers also offer local storage, which works similarly but is controlled by the site's code rather than sent with every request.

Essential Cookies

This website does not require cookies to function. Our hosting provider may set strictly necessary technical cookies or keep server logs for security and delivery; these are not used for advertising or profiling.

Analytics Cookies

The website currently does not use analytics cookies. If analytics are added in the future, this page will be updated first and, where required, your consent will be requested.

Advertising Cookies

The website currently does not serve third-party advertising and does not set advertising cookies. Reserved ad spaces are empty placeholders. If an advertising partner such as Google AdSense is activated later, that partner may set cookies as described in its own policies, and this page will be updated before activation.

Cookie Preferences

Your only stored preference is your theme choice (dark or light), kept in local storage on your device — not in a cookie, and never sent to us.

Cookie Management

You can view, block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time. Blocking cookies will not affect this website's core features. Clearing site data will also reset your theme preference.

Third-Party Cookies

External sites we link to, such as Pinterest, may set their own cookies when you visit them. Those are governed by the respective sites' policies, not ours.
