Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information PIN VIDEO DOWNLOADER ("we", "the website") handles when you visit or use the website, and the choices you have. The website is operated by the site owner ("the Owner"). Owner contact email: support@example.com (placeholder — the Owner will publish the real address here). By using the website you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

We designed this website to collect as little information as possible. We do not require accounts and we do not ask for your Pinterest password or any other third-party credentials. The information handled is limited to: (1) data you type or paste into tools on the site, processed as described below; (2) messages you send through the contact form, when the form service is connected; and (3) basic technical information that any web host receives automatically, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, in server logs.

URL Processing

Links you paste into the downloader are validated in your browser. In the standard frontend-only configuration, pasted links are not transmitted to or stored on our servers. If an authorized media integration is connected in the future, the link you submit would be sent to that service only to process your request, and this policy will be updated before that happens.

File Processing

All image tools run entirely in your browser using your device's processing power. Your image files are never uploaded to our servers, never stored by us, and are discarded by your browser when you close or reset the tool.

Cookies

The website itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies. A small number of strictly functional preferences are stored on your device (see Local Storage). If advertising or analytics are added in the future, additional cookies may be used as described in our Cookie Policy, and this policy will be updated.

Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage only to remember your dark/light theme preference. This stays on your device, is never transmitted to us, and you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Analytics

In its standard configuration the website does not run third-party analytics. If privacy-respecting analytics are introduced later, this section will describe what is measured and how to opt out.

Advertising

The website contains reserved advertising spaces in preparation for a possible future application to an advertising program such as Google AdSense. Until an advertising partner is active, these spaces display no third-party ads and set no advertising cookies.

Third-Party Services

The website may be hosted on a static hosting provider (for example Cloudflare Pages) and may use a form-handling service for the contact form. Those providers process data under their own privacy policies. Links to Pinterest and other external sites are governed by those sites' policies.

Data Security

The website is served over HTTPS. Because image processing and link validation happen on your device, your files and links are not exposed to our servers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We do not retain your links or image files. Contact-form messages, when the form is connected, are retained by the form provider and the Owner only as long as needed to respond and keep reasonable records, after which they are deleted.

User Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information about you, and to object to certain processing. Because we hold almost no personal information, most requests can be answered quickly. Contact us using the details below to exercise any right.

Children's Privacy

The website is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy when the website changes. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the website after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact Information

For any privacy question or request, contact the Owner at support@example.com (placeholder) or through the Contact Us page.
