Intellectual Property Rights

Videos, images and other content found through third-party platforms belong to their respective creators and rights holders. Nothing on this website transfers any ownership of such content to users.

Copyright Complaint Process

If you are a rights holder (or their authorized agent) and believe content accessible through this website infringes your copyright, you may send us a complaint. Because this website does not host user content and processes files only on visitors' own devices, complaints usually concern links or descriptive material, which we will review promptly.

Required Complaint Information

Please include: (1) your full name and contact email; (2) identification of the copyrighted work; (3) the specific URL or material on this website you are complaining about; (4) a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized; and (5) a statement that the information you provide is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorized to act for them.

Review Process

We review complete complaints in the order received, normally within a few business days. We may contact you if more information is needed.

Content Removal Procedure

Where a complaint is valid, we will remove or disable access to the material under our control and, where appropriate, take steps to prevent repeat issues. We will inform the complainant of the outcome.
