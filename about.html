About Us

The story and purpose behind PIN VIDEO DOWNLOADER.

Introduction

PIN VIDEO DOWNLOADER is an independent media utility website. It helps people work with media they own or are authorized to use: validating Pinterest links, previewing available media, downloading direct media files, and handling everyday image tasks — compressing, resizing, converting, cropping, creating PDFs and viewing metadata — directly in the browser.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: make small media tasks fast, private and honest. No sign-up walls, no misleading buttons, and no fake download links. If something cannot be done — for example, when a video is private or an authorized integration is not available — the site says so clearly.

Our Vision

We want to be a trustworthy toolbox that respects both users and creators: users get tools that work on any phone, and creators' rights are respected through an authorized-use-only approach to downloads.

What We Offer

  • A Pinterest link validation and preview workflow for authorized media.
  • Direct media file downloads for files you host or own.
  • Eight free image tools that process files entirely on your device.
  • Clear guides, honest status messages and a responsive, mobile-first design.

Why Choose Us

  • Privacy first: image tools never upload your files to a server.
  • No mandatory registration: open the site and use the tools.
  • Honest by design: real functionality only — never simulated results.
  • Works everywhere: Android, iPhone and desktop browsers.

Our Commitment to Privacy

We collect as little as possible. Links you paste are validated in your browser, your theme choice is stored only on your device, and image files stay on your device. Our Privacy Policy explains this in full.

Responsible Use

Downloading other people's content without permission can infringe copyright. PIN VIDEO DOWNLOADER is built for your own or authorized media, and we ask every visitor to respect creators' rights and Pinterest's terms. See our Copyright Policy.

Future Plans

We plan to add an authorized media integration for smoother Pinterest previews, more image tools, and performance improvements — always keeping the same privacy-first, honest approach.
